Assignment Name: AI Around Me
Description: List of 10 real-life AI applications used daily and how Machine Learning works behind them:

1.Google Search
Uses ML algorithms to rank search results based on user intent, keywords, and past search behavior.

2.YouTube
Recommendation systems use deep learning and collaborative filtering to suggest videos based on watch history.

3.Instagram
ML models analyze likes, comments, and watch time to personalize feed and reels.

4.Amazon
Uses ML for personalized product recommendations and predicting customer buying patterns.

5.Google Maps
Predicts traffic congestion and optimal routes using real-time and historical traffic data.

6.Gmail
Applies ML-based spam detection and Smart Reply using Natural Language Processing (NLP).

7.Netflix
Uses recommendation algorithms to suggest shows based on viewing history and user similarity.

8.Spotify
Applies ML to recommend songs and playlists using listening patterns and genre similarity.

9.Google Assistant
Uses speech recognition and NLP models to understand and respond to voice commands.

10.Flipkart
Uses ML for personalized shopping suggestions and fraud detection during online transactions.
